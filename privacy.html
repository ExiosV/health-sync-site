Privacy policy

health-sync is a personal, single-owner tool. It requests access to the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets only to create and update the owner's own files with the owner's own fitness and health data.

  • Google user data is used only for that purpose.
  • Data is stored only in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own server.
  • Data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the owner's Google Account permissions.
